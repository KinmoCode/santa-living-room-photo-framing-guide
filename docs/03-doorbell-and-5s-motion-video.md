# 03 — Front-Door Video Doorbells & Fixed-Mount 5-Second Motion Clips

## 1. Homes Without Chimneys: Video Doorbell Framing

For apartments, townhouses, and modern homes without fireplaces, kids naturally ask how Santa gets inside—and check the front-door camera first.

To frame an authentic [Front-Door Santa Camera photo](https://catchsantainmyhouse.com/front-door-santa-camera):
1. **Stand inside or step onto the walkway** depending on your doorbell orientation:
   - Most `Ring`, `Nest`, and `Blink` doorbells face **outward** from `1.15m–1.25m` beside the latch jamb toward the porch steps and walkway.
   - Hold your phone flat against the siding right next to the actual doorbell lens in horizontal (`16:9`) orientation.
2. **Porch Lighting**: Keep the porch sconce or holiday wreath lights switched **ON** (`2700K–3000K`) so the doormat, porch railing, and steps have natural night illumination without using phone flash.
3. **Leave Step Clearance**: Ensure at least `1.5m` (`5 ft`) of porch or front walk is visible so a figure carrying a burlap gift sack fits naturally without head cropping.

## 2. Fixed-Mount Rules for 5-Second Motion Clips

Real motion-triggered home cameras clip `5–12 seconds` of locked-off video when movement crosses a detection zone. When preparing a base room photo for a [5-second Caught-on-Camera motion video](https://catchsantainmyhouse.com/caught-santa-on-camera-video):

- **Zero Motion Blur in the Input Photo**: Because the room background stays locked like a wall-mounted camera, any handheld blur on ornaments or furniture in the input photo will conflict with moving foreground elements. Rest the phone against a doorframe or bookshelf before pressing the shutter.
- **Avoid Moving Ceiling Fans or TV Screens**: Pause any TV screen savers or spinning ceiling fans before snapping the base frame.
- **Provide a Clear Walk/Turn Path**: Frame `1.5m–2.0m` of visible floor between the room entrance/hearth and the Christmas tree so a 5-second tiptoe or gift-placing motion has natural spatial depth.
