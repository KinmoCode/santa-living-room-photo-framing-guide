# 01 — Camera Mounting Height, Tilt Angle & Focal Length

## Why Eye-Level Snapshots Break Security-Camera Realism

Residential indoor cameras (`Wyze Cam`, `Ring Indoor Cam`, `Blink Mini`, `Google Nest Cam`) are almost never mounted at standing adult eye height (`1.60m`) in the middle of a room. They sit in one of two places:

1. **Upper Corner / Top of Bookshelf (`2.0m – 2.3m`)** pitched downward `15°–25°` so the lens covers both the room entry and the floor around the Christmas tree.
2. **Console / Mantel Shelf (`1.1m – 1.35m`)** tucked against a wall looking diagonally across the living room.

When you take your source photo from one of those two positions, verticals converge naturally and the floor plane opens up, giving a compositor enough carpet or hardwood geometry to anchor boot contact shadows accurately.

## Recommended Phone Lens & Distance Matrix

| Room Size | Phone Lens Setting | Equivalent Focal Length | Distance to Tree / Hearth | Framing Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Compact Apartment Living Room (`< 3.5m`)** | `0.5×` Ultra-Wide | `13mm – 16mm` | `2.0m – 2.8m` | Keep the tree at least `15%` inward from frame edges to avoid heavy barrel stretch |
| **Standard Living / Family Room (`3.5m – 6m`)** | `1×` Main Wide | `24mm – 26mm` | `3.0m – 4.5m` | Best low-light sensor size and cleanest night grain |
| **Hallway Looking Toward Tree / Stockings** | `1×` Main Wide | `24mm – 26mm` | `3.5m – 5.5m` | Partial doorway frame on left/right adds authentic "hidden camera" context |
| **Front Porch / Entryway** | `0.5×` or `1×` | `16mm – 24mm` | `1.5m – 2.5m` | Hold phone right beside doorbell plate (`1.2m` off porch floor) |

*Avoid `2×` or `3×` telephoto lenses indoors at night—phones often crop digitally when lux drops below `20 lx`, producing muddy blotches instead of natural camera grain.*
