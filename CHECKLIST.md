# Christmas Eve Room-Shot Checklist (2-Minute Parent Prep)

Run through these 8 checks on Christmas Eve after the kids are asleep and before taking your room or porch photo:

- [ ] **1. Wipe the rear phone camera lens** with a soft cloth so Christmas tree bulbs glow cleanly without oily streak flares.
- [ ] **2. Switch to horizontal landscape (`16:9` or `4:3`)** using the main `1×` lens (`24mm–26mm` equiv.) or `0.5×` ultra-wide in tight rooms.
- [ ] **3. Turn OFF overhead ceiling lights**; keep Christmas tree string lights, fireplace garland, or a dim hallway nightlight **ON**.
- [ ] **4. Elevate the camera** (`1.9m–2.3m` high bookshelf/doorframe for corner-cam look, or `1.2m` mantel/doorbell height) with a slight downward tilt.
- [ ] **5. Clear a `1.2m × 1.2m` (`4 ft × 4 ft`) floor zone** next to the tree skirt, fireplace stockings, or milk-and-cookies table.
- [ ] **6. Check mirror, window, and TV reflections** to make sure nobody holding a phone is visible in dark glass surfaces.
- [ ] **7. Verify wrapping-paper continuity** so any presents visible in the midnight frame match what kids see on Christmas morning.
- [ ] **8. Brace elbows or rest the phone on a shelf** for `1 second` if phone Night Mode activates, keeping furniture edges sharp.
