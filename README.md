## Quick 60-Second Reference Matrix

| Check | Real Home Security Camera Cue | How to Stage Your Room Snapshot |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Camera Elevation** | Corner shelf or upper wall (`2.0m – 2.3m`), tilted `15°–25°` down | Hold phone near bookshelf height or prop on a hallway cabinet—avoid `1.5m` eye-level portrait framing |
| **Focal Length / FOV** | Fixed wide lens (`110°–130°` diagonal, `16mm–26mm` equiv.) | Use phone `1×` (`24mm–26mm`) or `0.5×` (`13mm–16mm`) in horizontal landscape (`16:9` or `4:3`) |
| **Overhead Ceiling Lights** | Switched **OFF** overnight | Turn off ceiling cans; leave only Christmas tree string lights (`2400K–2700K`) or a dim hallway nightlight |
| **Floor Clearance** | Unobstructed rug or hardwood path (`1.2m × 1.2m`) | Leave clear floor space beside the tree skirt or cookie plate for boots and contact shadows |
| **Reflections** | Dark TV glass and framed windows reflect camera operators | Stand slightly off-axis from blank TV screens, mirrors, and patio glass |
| **Gift Continuity** | Wrapping paper in midnight capture matches 7:00 AM morning room | Snap the room photo either before placing Santa's gifts or after setting out the exact morning presents |
